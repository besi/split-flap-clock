![](assets/logo.png)

Driver board for four 28BYJ-48 stepper motors.

## Pinout

Motors: 

| Pins        | 1  | 2  | 3  | 4  | Hall  |
|-------------|----|----|----|----|-------|
| **Motor A** | 10 | 9  | 3  | 8  | 18 (7)|
| **Motor B** | 13 | 14 | 21 | 11 | 12    |
| **Motor C** | 40 | 39 | 38 | 6  | 47    |
| **Motor D** | 1  | 2  | 42 | 41 | 48    |

Other GPIOs

| Function       | Pin |
|----------------|-----|
| DCF-77         | 4   |
| SDA / Switch B | 16  |
| SCL / Switch A | 17  |
| Mode           | 0   |
| Neopixel       | 15  |
| Temperature    | 5   |
